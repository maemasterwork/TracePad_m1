# Learner guide (English)

## Start in one minute
Open Tracepad and choose **Course**, then the lesson. You do not need an account: you are a *guest* and your progress is saved on this device only (the badge at the top says so).

## Reading and running code
Each lesson is split into chapters (use the Contents list). Every code example has a **Run** button (or press Ctrl+Enter inside the code). The result appears under the code.
- **Stop** ends a program that is stuck, for example `while True: pass`. A program is also stopped automatically after a time limit.
- If a program asks for input (`input()`), a box appears under the code; type your answer and press Enter.
- You can change the code and run it again. Your changes are not saved to the lesson itself.
- Files your programs write stay on this device and are there next time you run.
- The first run downloads the Python engine (about 12 MB). After that it also works with no internet.

## Marking chapters as read
Press **Mark as read** at the bottom of a chapter. Chapters you finished show a tick in the Contents list.

## An account (optional)
Choose **Create account**. Say how old you are; if you are under 13 you cannot make an account yourself, so ask a parent or your teacher; the administrator creates the account for you. If you have no email, tick "I have no email address" and choose a username (letters, numbers, dots and underscores). Your guest progress moves into your account the first time you sign in. Your work then follows you to any device.
In **Account** you can change your name, password and email, sign out other devices, turn on two-factor sign-in, download all your data, or delete your account. Deleting is permanent.
If you forget your password and have an email, use **Forgot password**. If you signed up with a username only, ask your teacher or the administrator.

## Settings
**Settings** changes the language (English or فارسی), theme (light, dark, high contrast), text size (80 to 200 %), a colour-blind-friendly palette, a dyslexia-friendly font, line spacing and reduced motion. Choices stay on your device.

## Good to know

- Your marks and results are saved by your browser. Teachers will see them as *self-reported*.
